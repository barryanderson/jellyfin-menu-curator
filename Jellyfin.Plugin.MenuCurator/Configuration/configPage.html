Menu Curator

Choose which plugin pages appear in the dashboard menu.

Changes can take up to a minute to appear in the menu. If they have not appeared by then, reload the dashboard.

Menu pages

Untick a page to remove it from the menu. Its settings stay available from Dashboard > Plugins.

Rules for pages that are not loaded

These rules refer to pages from plugins that are disabled, removed or renamed. They have no effect until the page returns. Untick a rule to delete it.

Plugins with no settings page

These plugins register no page, so they cannot appear in the menu.
